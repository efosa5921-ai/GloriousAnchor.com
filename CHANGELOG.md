## 26 October 2026

- **The "From our writers" slider now always opens on the newest article**, with the rest of the last 30 days' articles behind it, newest first; a page left open moves on one slide every two hours. Older articles leave the slider and stay under Our Articles.

## 25 October 2026

- **Two new articles**: [Purpose and Ambition: a juxtaposition](/articles/purpose-and-ambition-a-juxtaposition/) by Efosa Adun, and [Dr Paul Enenche: the doctor who left medicine to build Dunamis](/articles/paul-enenche-doctor-who-left-medicine-for-ministry/), the story of two doctors who left medicine in 1996, with his YouTube and social handles.
- **New article: [The Ark at Canaanland: the history, progress and opening date of Winners' Chapel's 109,000-seat church](/articles/the-ark-canaanland-winners-chapel/)**, with three videos and photographs of the building.
- Every article now carries a closing "Questions this article answers" section, links to related sections, and tabs: the Noah, GAIN TV and welcome articles were brought up to the same standard as the newer ones. The Noah article gains a section on Noah's 120 years of warning (Genesis 6:3, 2 Peter 2:5, Matthew 24:38-39).

## 24 October 2026

- **New article: [Prophet Isa El-Buba: the former Islamic occultist who says he saw hell](/articles/prophet-isa-el-buba-former-muslim-occultist-turned-prophet/)**, with his video testimony playing on the page. Articles can now carry an embedded video.
- **New article: [Jordan Church attack: Islamist gunmen kill eight Christians at a funeral in Karak](/articles/jordan-church-attack-karak-funeral/)**: what happened at St George's Church in Hmoud, who did it, Jordan's response, and how to pray.
- **"From our writers" slider** leads Top Stories: every Glorious Anchor article in one slider, opening on a different article every two hours, with arrows, dots and swipe to flick through the rest. Pinned items and strong stories fill the slots beside it.
- **Pins now expire.** A pinned article or pick leads Top Stories for three days, then takes its normal place by date, so the front page no longer holds on to an old story. The Liatu Samuel Dawa article and post are unpinned.
- Videos from channels that switch off embedding (RHEMA, for one) no longer disappear from the site: they were being mistaken for private videos.
- **Three more channels**: Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners Chapel, starred), Apostle Arome Osayi (The Holy Spirit tab) and RHEMA with Kenneth E. Hagin (Teaching).

## 23 October 2026

- **The site now leans charismatic, Pentecostal and evangelistic.** 46 new sources: Pentecostal news (Faith News Network, RCCG) and persecution reporting (Morning Star News, International Christian Concern); Joyce Meyer teaching; YouTube channels from the Billy Graham Evangelistic Association, Christ For All Nations, Greg Laurie, Nick Vujicic, Living Waters, Joseph Prince, Joel Osteen, Transformation Church, Bethel Church, Bill Johnson, Jesus Image, David Diga Hernandez, Isaiah Saldivar, Vlad Savchuk, Koinonia Global, Dunamis TV, Jerry Eze (NSPPD), TBN, GOD TV and Charisma Media; and 19 podcasts on GAIN Radio including Joseph Prince, Jentezen Franklin, Joyce Meyer, Believer's Voice of Victory, T.D. Jakes, Steven Furtick, Apostle Joshua Selman, Bishop David Oyedepo, Priscilla Shirer and John Bevere. Catholic, Reformed and progressive sources are paused (they can be switched back on in the admin).
- **Pick**: Frank Turek answers a student who asked why her 'queer' behaviour is considered wrong, live on campus (video, in Apologetics). A pick can now be marked as a video or audio when its link does not say so, so the card reads Watch or Listen.

## 21 October 2026

- **"Copy post" button** on every article and story page: copies the headline, summary and link in one go, ready to paste into a WhatsApp channel or group without typing the headline (WhatsApp's channel composer on iPhone can lose text typed above a link preview).
- **New article: [Liatu Samuel Dawa, the Christian mother jailed 14 years in Borno for sharing her faith](/articles/liatu-samuel-dawa-jailed-14-years-in-borno/)**: what the court ruled, the charge, her plea, her husband in hiding, what Nigeria's Constitution says, and how to pray.
- Cards and article pictures now favour the top of a photo, so faces in portrait pictures are no longer cut off.

## 19 October 2026

- **Every section has its own address** (for example [/eschatology/](/eschatology/), [/news/](/news/), [/holyspirit/](/holyspirit/), [/writers/](/writers/)), with a short introduction, so search engines can list each section and you can link straight to one. All sections are linked in the footer.
- **Digest for WhatsApp, with pictures**: "Share as picture" now makes one tall picture with every story's photo and headline, and a new "Post story by story" list copies each story on its own so every WhatsApp post gets its own preview picture.
- **No more adverts as story pictures**: pictures scraped from an article's own text are filtered for ads, banners and logos, and replaced by the picture the publisher chose for sharing whenever the page offers one.
- **Share buttons on every article and story page**: WhatsApp, X, Facebook, Telegram, email, copy link, and your phone's own share sheet.
- YouTube channels load more reliably: the build retries when YouTube's feed server stumbles, which it has been doing often, and can fall back to YouTube's official data service once a free key is added.
- **Prophecy Watchers**, **Prophecy News Watch** and **Endtime Ministries** join the Prophecy in the News tab: Endtime's articles and all three YouTube channels (the videos also appear under YouTube).
- New tab, **Prophecy in the News**: current events read in the light of Scripture.
- Our own articles can now appear in other tabs too (for example Eschatology), chosen in the admin's article editor.
- Videos that a channel has since made private or deleted (usually a livestream after it ended) no longer linger on the site, and a video that has no picture yet shows the branded cover tile instead of YouTube's grey placeholder.

## 18 October 2026

- Posts to X now carry the story's picture.
- Picks can now sit in any tab, not just Picks: the first is a video on the Holy Spirit in **The Holy Spirit** tab. Video and audio picks say "Watch" or "Listen" on their story page and show up under the "Video only" and "Audio only" filters.
- **Share the digest as a picture**: a branded card of the day's headlines for WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, with the text copied for the caption.
- **Follow us**: the Glorious Anchor WhatsApp channel and Telegram channel are linked under the subscribe box and in the footer.
- **The Holy Spirit** tab: news and teaching on the Spirit-filled life from Charisma News, Charisma Magazine, The Pneuma Review and Influence Magazine.

## 17 October 2026

- New Bible quiz: **The Holy Spirit**, ten referenced questions, with its video.
- **Bible Quiz** at [/quiz/](/quiz/): a quiz of the day plus a library of ten-question quizzes, with the Bible reference shown after each answer and a score you can share. Reach it from the tab bar or the app shortcut. New quizzes are written in the admin, and each quiz can be turned into a vertical video for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels or WhatsApp status with one click.
- A **Contact** link in the footer: info@gloriousanchor.com.
- **Subscribe by email**: a sign-up box on the front page, the digest and every story page. The digest arrives each morning; free, no spam, leave any time.
- **Pictures for more stories**: when a feed leaves a story without a picture, we fetch the one its own page names for sharing. Stories that still have none get a designed cover with the source's name instead of a blank.
- **Share from any card**: WhatsApp, X, Facebook, Telegram, email or copy link, and your phone's own share sheet on mobile. Shared stories use a short gloriousanchor.com link that opens a story page here, with the headline, a "read the full story" button and more from the same section.
- **Daily digest** at [/digest/](/digest/): the day's best stories as picture cards, with a one-tap "Copy for WhatsApp" and an email-ready feed. Reach it from the "Daily digest" tab or the app's home-screen shortcut.
- **RSS feeds**: [/feed.xml](/feed.xml) for the latest items and [/digest/feed.xml](/digest/feed.xml) for one digest a day.
- New items can be **posted automatically to a Telegram channel and to X** once those accounts are connected.
- Optional **visitor statistics** (GoatCounter or Cloudflare, both cookie-free) switched on from the admin settings.
- **gaintv.app** and **gainradio.app** now open GAIN TV and GAIN Radio directly.

## 16 October 2026

- **GAIN TV** and **GAIN Radio**: branded front doors for the Glorious Anchor International Network at [/tv/](/tv/) and [/radio/](/radio/). GAIN TV opens on the video section; GAIN Radio opens on the new **Radio** tab.
- **Radio** tab: Christian podcasts and audio teaching, with a Listen button that plays each episode right on the card and shows its length. An "Audio only" filter joins the sort menu.
- **Apologetics** and **Eschatology** tabs, seeded with trusted sites and channels. A source can now appear in more than one tab.
- Faster loading: the front page becomes usable after one small download, and thumbnails are resized on the fly so they cost a few dozen kilobytes instead of megabytes.
- Search-engine groundwork: sitemap, structured data on every page type, pre-rendered headlines for crawlers, and a preview card when links are shared.
- The welcome article now describes every section and feature.

## 15 October 2026

- **Find a church**: a finder for churches near you or near any town, with distances, a map, directions, and churches listed directly with Glorious Anchor shown first. Each listed church gets its own page.
- **Our Articles**: original writing published on the site, with its own tab, pages and feed.
- **Picks**: hand-chosen links, including single articles, X posts, TikToks, videos and Instagram or Facebook posts, with an editor's note.
- An admin page for managing picks, sources, articles, churches and settings from any browser.

## 14 October 2026

- The site moved to its own address, gloriousanchor.com, with HTTPS.
- Self-healing feeds: wrong addresses are rediscovered from the publisher's homepage, and failing sources are reported automatically.

## 11 October 2026

- Glorious Anchor launched: Christian news, faith and culture writing, teaching and devotionals, personal writers, YouTube channels and social communities on one page.
- **My Feed**: follow only the sources you want, with choices kept on your device.
- Install it as an app on your phone, with offline reading.
