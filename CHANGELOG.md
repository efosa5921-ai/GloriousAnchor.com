## 17 October 2026

- **Pictures for more stories**: when a feed leaves a story without a picture, we fetch the one its own page names for sharing. Stories that still have none get a designed cover with the source's name instead of a blank.
- **Share from any card**: WhatsApp, X, Facebook, Telegram, email or copy link, and your phone's own share sheet on mobile. Shared stories use a short gloriousanchor.com link that opens a story page here, with the headline, a "read the full story" button and more from the same section.
- **Daily digest** at [/digest/](/digest/): the day's best stories in one short list, with a one-tap "Copy for WhatsApp" and an email-ready feed.
- **RSS feeds**: [/feed.xml](/feed.xml) for the latest items and [/digest/feed.xml](/digest/feed.xml) for one digest a day.
- New items can be **posted automatically to a Telegram channel and to X** once those accounts are connected.
- Optional **visitor statistics** (GoatCounter or Cloudflare, both cookie-free) switched on from the admin settings.
- **gaintv.app** and **gainradio.app** now open GAIN TV and GAIN Radio directly.

## 16 October 2026

- **GAIN TV** and **GAIN Radio**: branded front doors for the Glorious Anchor International Network at [/tv/](/tv/) and [/radio/](/radio/). GAIN TV opens on the video section; GAIN Radio opens on the new **Radio** tab.
- **Radio** tab: Christian podcasts and audio teaching, with a Listen button that plays each episode right on the card and shows its length. An "Audio only" filter joins the sort menu.
- **Apologetics** and **Eschatology** tabs, seeded with trusted sites and channels. A source can now appear in more than one tab.
- Faster loading: the front page becomes usable after one small download, and thumbnails are resized on the fly so they cost a few dozen kilobytes instead of megabytes.
- Search-engine groundwork: sitemap, structured data on every page type, pre-rendered headlines for crawlers, and a preview card when links are shared.
- The welcome article now describes every section and feature.

## 15 October 2026

- **Find a church**: a finder for churches near you or near any town, with distances, a map, directions, and churches listed directly with Glorious Anchor shown first. Each listed church gets its own page.
- **Our Articles**: original writing published on the site, with its own tab, pages and feed.
- **Picks**: hand-chosen links, including single articles, X posts, TikToks, videos and Instagram or Facebook posts, with an editor's note.
- An admin page for managing picks, sources, articles, churches and settings from any browser.

## 14 October 2026

- The site moved to its own address, gloriousanchor.com, with HTTPS.
- Self-healing feeds: wrong addresses are rediscovered from the publisher's homepage, and failing sources are reported automatically.

## 11 October 2026

- Glorious Anchor launched: Christian news, faith and culture writing, teaching and devotionals, personal writers, YouTube channels and social communities on one page.
- **My Feed**: follow only the sources you want, with choices kept on your device.
- Install it as an app on your phone, with offline reading.
