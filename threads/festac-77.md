January 1977: one naira bought $1.55.

In 1974 Nigeria had lent the IMF $120m and the World Bank $240m. External debt: about $3bn. We owed nobody anything we could not pay.
---
15 January to 12 February 1977: a priest of Shango lit the festival fire at Surulere. The head of state poured libation to the ancestors. The emblem was the Queen Idia mask. Bori dancers rode with the emirs at Kaduna.
---
January 1978: the first $1bn commercial loan.
1982: $18.6bn in debt.
1986: the naira floats.
June 2026: ₦1,379 to the dollar and ₦167 trillion in debt. Nigeria now owes the World Bank over $20bn.
