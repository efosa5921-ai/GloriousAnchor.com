TITLE
FESTAC 77 at 50: The Four Weeks Nigeria Was Leased to Darkness, and the Jubilee of 2027

DESCRIPTION
On 15 January 1977 a priest of Shango lit the festival fire at the National Stadium, Surulere, and FESTAC 77 began. That month one naira bought $1.55, and Nigeria had just lent money to the IMF and the World Bank. Fifty years later a dollar costs ₦1,379 and the nation owes ₦167 trillion.

This film tells the whole story: what Nigeria had before the festival, what was done in Lagos and Kaduna in the nation's name, what Scripture says about it, the fall that followed, why the fifty-year lease ends on 12 February 2027, why the 2027 elections were moved to 16 January and 6 February, and the two jubilees Nigeria must choose between.
