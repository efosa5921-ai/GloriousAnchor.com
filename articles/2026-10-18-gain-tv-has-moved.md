If you followed **GAIN TV on WhatsApp**, you may have noticed that the channel is gone. WhatsApp closed it, and we cannot post there or message its followers any more. Nothing else has changed: GAIN TV continues, with more than before.

## Where GAIN TV lives now

- **[gaintv.app](https://gaintv.app)**: new videos every day from Christian teaching, apologetics and worship channels, on one fast page. No app to install and no sign-up. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.
- **[GAIN Radio](https://gainradio.app)**: Christian podcasts and audio teaching you can play right on the page.
- **[Glorious Anchor](https://gloriousanchor.com)**: the whole network in one place, with Christian news, teaching, articles, a daily digest, the Bible quiz and a church finder.

## Follow the new channels

- **WhatsApp:** [join the Glorious Anchor channel](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbDsSvP2Jl86RiDqNe3d), or open WhatsApp, go to Updates, and search for "Glorious Anchor".
- **Telegram:** [t.me/gloriousanchor](https://t.me/gloriousanchor) gets every new story and video automatically.
- **X:** [@GloriousAnchor](https://x.com/GloriousAnchor).
- **Email:** the [daily digest](https://gloriousanchor.com/digest/) brings the day's best stories to your inbox each morning.
