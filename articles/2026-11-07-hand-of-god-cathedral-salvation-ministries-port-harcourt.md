> *The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former, saith the LORD of hosts: and in this place will I give peace, saith the LORD of hosts.* (Haggai 2:9)

On Friday 6 November 2026, at ten in the morning, Bishop David Oyedepo stood on a platform at Kilometre 10 of the Port Harcourt airport road and dedicated a building shaped like an open hand. Around him, by the church's count, were seats for 120,000 people. The Hand of God Cathedral, the new world headquarters of Salvation Ministries, had taken sixteen years to build. Its founder, Pastor David Ibiyeomie, told the crowd it had been paid for without a single loan.

If the seat count holds, and no outside body has yet measured it, it is the largest church auditorium on earth, larger than the Glory Dome in Abuja, larger than the Ark that Oyedepo's own Winners Chapel is finishing at Canaanland, larger than anything in Seoul or São Paulo or Houston. The first Sunday service is tomorrow, 8 November. This is what was built, who built it, what was said over it, and what a building this size is for.

*Picture: the Hand of God Cathedral, the central dome and its five wings, as rendered for the dedication broadcast. Salvation Ministries.*

## From 34 people on Birabi Street

Salvation Ministries began on 13 April 1997 with 34 people in a small building at 35 Birabi Street in the Government Reservation Area of Port Harcourt. David Ibiyeomie, then in his mid-thirties, had been a member of Oyedepo's Living Faith Church and still calls the bishop his father in the faith. The church grew the way Port Harcourt grew in the oil years: fast, loud and in every direction. By February 2011 it was opening fourteen satellite churches on a single day, each running five services, and today it counts its branches in the thousands, in Nigeria and abroad. Its annual gatherings, 5 Nights of Glory and Glory Reign, fill stadiums; its television programme, Hour of Salvation, runs across Nigeria and overseas.

The headquarters could not keep up. In 2010 the church began a new auditorium at Igwuruta, on the road out to the airport, planned for 40,000 seats. Somewhere in the building of it the plan tripled.

## The building

The cathedral covers about 56,130 square metres, roughly eight football pitches under one roof. Its plan is a hand laid palm down: a central dome and five wings reaching out from it like fingers, which is where the name comes from, and why some people call it the Five Fingers of God. It rises eight floors. Reports speak of some 400 toilets and parking for 350 vehicles, the last of which cannot be the whole story for a crowd of this size and probably describes one car park of several.

A Chinese firm did the roof. By October 2021 the roofing and plastering were finished and the church was promising a dedication at the first Glory Reign of January 2022. It took almost five more years. A report dated November 2025 announced the dedication as already done; it was wrong, or early. The ceremony happened this Friday.

Ibiyeomie told the congregation that a construction firm asked to assess the project had valued the structure alone at ₦46 billion, about 213 million dollars at the exchange rate of the time. Then he said the thing the whole crowd had come to hear: "The entire Hand of God Cathedral project is debt-free, no loans and no foreign aid." It was built, he said, from the giving of the members.

## The land that was nearly taken

He also told a story most of the crowd had not heard. Twice, in 2008 and again in 2010, the Rivers State government moved to take back the land at Igwuruta on which the cathedral now stands. He refused to give it up. He did not name the officials involved, and did not need to; everyone in Rivers knows which administration held power in those years. The point he drew was not political. The land was contested, the church prayed and held on, and the building stands where it was threatened.

## Fathers and sons

The platform on Friday was a family tree of Nigerian Pentecostalism. Oyedepo, who dedicated the building, was himself raised in ministry under Archbishop Benson Idahosa, whose Faith Arena in Benin City, dedicated in November 1986, was Africa's first "stadium church" at 10,000 seats. Idahosa's son, Bishop Feb Idahosa, sat on the platform. So did Pastor Paul Enenche of Dunamis, another of Oyedepo's spiritual sons, whose 100,000-seat Glory Dome in Abuja held the record the Hand of God has now taken, and who was gracious enough to come and watch it go. Bishop Mike Okonkwo, Archbishop Wale Oke, Pastor Jerry Eze, Pastor Korede Komaiya, Pastor Steve Ogah, Pastor Charles Osazuwa and Pastor Goodman Alabi were there, with Faith Oyedepo and Peace Ibiyeomie. Peter Obi came from politics. There is no report of the Rivers State governor attending.

Ibiyeomie spoke about the bishop as a son speaks: "Every time I get to a crossroad, I position myself to hear from Bishop Oyedepo and he always says the thing I want answers to." A clip of Jerry Eze going down on his knees to greet first Ibiyeomie and then Oyedepo went round the internet by evening, as did the footage of the bishops seeing Oyedepo off to his jet. Nigerians, as ever, were divided about what they saw.

## "This will never be reduced to an altar of entertainment"

Oyedepo's dedication prayer was short and it was pointed. "The fire of the Lord will never go out from this altar," he declared. "This will never be reduced to an altar of entertainment." Anyone who has watched a great church lose its first love will know why a man of 72 would pray that over a building meant to outlast him. He also spoke to the moment Nigeria is in, warning against any attempt to declare the country an Islamic state and saying the church would prevail.

## What is a 120,000-seat church for?

Solomon asked the question first, at the dedication of a far smaller house: "But will God indeed dwell on the earth? behold, the heaven and heaven of heavens cannot contain thee; how much less this house that I have builded?" (1 Kings 8:27). God does not need the Hand of God Cathedral. The people of Port Harcourt might.

The answer the critics give is that ₦46 billion could have built hospitals. It could. It could also have been spent, as it so often is in Nigeria, on things that would have done nobody any good, and this was given freely, by members, to a work they believe in, without a kobo of debt left for their children to carry. The answer the church gives is simpler: 120,000 seats means 120,000 people, several times each Sunday, hearing the gospel preached and being prayed for. Salvation Ministries has, over the years, funded a thousand scholarships, given to the teaching hospital in Port Harcourt, and begun a university on 200 hectares. A building is not the work. It is a place to do the work.

Haggai's word to the men rebuilding the temple was that the glory of the latter house would be greater than the former, not because it was bigger, but because "in this place will I give peace." That is the thing to pray for Igwuruta: that the fire does not go out, that the altar stays an altar, and that the 120,000 who sit down on Sunday stand up having met God. To him be the glory.

## Questions this article answers

### How big is the Hand of God Cathedral?

Salvation Ministries says the cathedral seats 120,000 people on a footprint of about 56,130 square metres, with a central dome and five wings in the shape of an open hand, over eight floors. The seat count has not been independently verified, but if it holds it is the largest church auditorium in the world.

### When was the Hand of God Cathedral dedicated and by whom?

Bishop David Oyedepo of Living Faith Church Worldwide dedicated it on Friday 6 November 2026 at Kilometre 10, Port Harcourt International Airport Road, Igwuruta, Rivers State. The first Sunday service is on 8 November 2026.

### How much did the Hand of God Cathedral cost?

Pastor David Ibiyeomie said an assessing firm valued the structure alone at ₦46 billion, about 213 million US dollars at the time, and that the project was "debt-free, no loans and no foreign aid", paid for by members' giving.

### Is the Hand of God Cathedral bigger than the Glory Dome and the Ark?

By the reported figures, yes. The Glory Dome in Abuja seats 100,000 and the Ark at Canaanland, still under construction, is planned for about 109,000. The Hand of God Cathedral's 120,000 would exceed both.

### Where is Salvation Ministries' headquarters now?

The Hand of God Cathedral at Igwuruta on the Port Harcourt airport road is the new global headquarters. The church was founded on 13 April 1997 at Birabi Street, GRA, Port Harcourt, with 34 members.
