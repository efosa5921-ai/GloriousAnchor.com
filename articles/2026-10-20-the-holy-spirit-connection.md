> *Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant.* (1 Corinthians 12:1)

These are my notes from **The Holy Spirit Connection**, the October special of the Leadership Certificate Course of the Word of Faith Bible Institute (WOFBI) at Winners' Chapel International, Atlanta. The course theme is "espousing diversities of operation of the Holy Spirit for impactful stewardship," taught in three modules covering fourteen operations of the Spirit. This article covers Module 1 and the first five operations of Module 2. The references are the ones given in class; read them with your Bible open, because the notes are only a map to the verses.

## The course at a glance

- **Module 1: The seven Spirits of Christ.** The Comforter, the gifts, and the seven Spirits upon Christ.
- **Module 2: Diverse manifestations of the Holy Ghost, part 1.** Seven operations: prayer and supplication, wisdom and revelation, faith, love, obedience, might, servanthood.
- **Module 3: Diverse manifestations of the Holy Ghost, part 2.** Seven operations: the fear of the Lord, truth, power, endurance, joy, grace, glory.

## Module 1: The seven Spirits of Christ

### The Comforter, our Helper

1. **The word "comforter" literally means helper.** Jesus promised "another Comforter" who would abide with us, teach us and bring his words to our remembrance (John 14:16-18, 26), and he said it was to our advantage that he go away so that the Helper would come (John 16:7-11, 12-14).
2. **The Holy Spirit comes down with diverse gifts** that help believers fulfil our glorious destiny in redemption (Romans 8:29-30).
3. **Until one is born again, he can never be baptised in the Holy Ghost** with the evidence of speaking with new tongues. The pattern is the same at Pentecost, at the house of Cornelius and at Ephesus (Acts 2:37-41; Acts 10:44-47; Acts 19:1-6).

### From new birth to the gifts

4. **Until one is baptised in the Holy Ghost, he cannot demand the gifts of the Holy Spirit.** Paul tells a Spirit-filled church to "covet earnestly the best gifts" (1 Corinthians 12:31).
5. **Apart from the nine gifts of the Holy Spirit, there are other ministries of the Holy Ghost available to us,** such as the Spirit of faith. "There are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit... diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all" (1 Corinthians 12:4-7; 2 Corinthians 4:13).

### The seven Spirits upon Christ

Isaiah saw the Spirit of the Lord resting on the Branch from the stem of Jesse, and John saw the Lamb with "seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth" (Isaiah 11:1-2; Revelation 5:6). The seven Spirits upon Christ include:

1. **The Spirit of the Lord**, which is power (Isaiah 11:1; Luke 4:14, 18).
2. **The Spirit of wisdom, knowledge, understanding and counsel** (Isaiah 11:1-2; Ephesians 1:17-18).
3. **The Spirit of might** (Isaiah 11:1; Ephesians 3:16).
4. **The Spirit of the fear of the Lord** (Isaiah 11:1-2; Romans 1:4).

### Why the Holy Ghost is not optional

1. **As the body without the spirit is dead, so is a believer without the Holy Ghost at work in him** (James 2:26).
2. **Our glorious destiny in redemption cannot be realised by power or might, but by the Spirit of God at work in us.** "Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, saith the LORD of hosts" (Zechariah 4:1-6).
3. **We need an unquenchable thirst to be endued with the empowerment of the Spirit.** David's soul thirsted for God in a dry land; Jesus said, "If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink," and John explains that he spoke of the Spirit (Psalm 63:1-3; John 7:37-39; 1 Corinthians 12:31).

### Crave the enduement

It is high time we began to crave the enduement of the diverse ministries of the Holy Spirit in our lives, in order to make the most of our adventure in the Kingdom (1 Corinthians 12:4-7). Beyond the nine gifts, Scripture names other operations of the Holy Spirit here and there, and the course looks at fourteen of them. This is the bridge into Module 2.

## Module 2: Diverse manifestations of the Holy Ghost, part 1

### 1. The Spirit of prayer and supplication

Key texts: Zechariah 12:10; Romans 8:26-27.

1. **The Holy Spirit keeps us spiritually alive and fervent in prayer.** "Watch and pray"; "fervent in spirit... continuing instant in prayer" (Matthew 26:41; Romans 12:11-12).
2. **The Holy Spirit empowers us to pray according to the will of God.** He "maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God," and prayers according to his will are heard (Romans 8:27; 1 John 5:14-15).
3. **Praying in the Holy Ghost connects us to the word in season,** which illuminates our spirit man for conquest (Jude 20; 1 Corinthians 14:14-15; 2 Corinthians 12:7).

### 2. The Spirit of wisdom and revelation

Key texts: Isaiah 11:1-3; Ephesians 1:17-20; Isaiah 60:1-3.

1. **It is the move of the Holy Spirit within our spirit that empowers our access into the deep things of God,** the things behind the veil. "There is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding"; "the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God" (Job 32:7-8; Romans 8:16; 1 Corinthians 2:9-10).
2. **The Holy Spirit imparts the Spirit of wisdom, which enhances our capacity to draw insight from what we see, hear, read or study.** Solomon drew wisdom from the ant and the field; Daniel understood by books (Proverbs 6:6-9; Proverbs 24:27-30; 1 Kings 4:29-34; Daniel 9:2).
3. **We need the Spirit of wisdom and revelation to accomplish any given task.** "If the iron be blunt... wisdom is profitable to direct" (Ecclesiastes 10:10).

### 3. The Spirit of faith

Key texts: John 6:26-28; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Jude 3, 20.

1. **The Holy Ghost deepens our insight into the word, which keeps our faith on fire for conquest.** Jesus prayed that Peter's faith would not fail; "this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith"; the shield of faith quenches every fiery dart (Luke 22:31-32; 1 Corinthians 2:10-13; 1 John 5:4; Hebrews 11:32-34; Ephesians 6:16).
2. **As our faith comes alive, we are enabled to command signs and wonders.** "This is the work of God, that ye believe on him whom he hath sent" (John 6:28-29).
3. **The Spirit of faith is the same from generation to generation.** "We having the same spirit of faith... we also believe, and therefore speak" (2 Corinthians 4:13).

### 4. The Spirit of love

Key texts: 2 Timothy 1:6-7; 1 Corinthians 13:1-3; 1 Corinthians 2:9.

1. **To walk in perfect love is to be filled with all the fullness of God,** which makes us more than conquerors over the battles of life. The three Hebrews loved God more than their lives and walked out of the fire (Ephesians 3:17-19; Daniel 3:14-28).
2. **Every addicted lover of God is ordained a pathfinder, pacesetter and trailblazer.** "Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard... the things which God hath prepared for them that love him"; love never fails (1 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Corinthians 13:8).
3. **We need the Spirit of love to sustain our love for God under all circumstances.** Peter denied the Lord three times in one night, yet filled with the Spirit he stood boldly before the council (Matthew 26:34, 69-75; Acts 4:13, 20).

### 5. The Spirit of obedience

Key texts: Ezekiel 36:27; Job 36:11; John 14:21.

1. **The Bible recognises the place and power of the spirit of obedience.** "I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes," set against "the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience" (Ezekiel 36:27; Ephesians 2:2).
2. **The spirit of obedience is a vital spiritual weapon with the capacity to win any war.** Paul's weapons are mighty through God, "having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience is fulfilled" (2 Corinthians 10:3-6).
3. **The Spirit of obedience makes obeying God a delight,** which causes the giant in one to rise. "Blessed is the man that feareth the LORD, that delighteth greatly in his commandments"; his delight is in the law of the LORD, and whatever he does prospers (Psalm 112:1-3; Psalm 1:2-3).
