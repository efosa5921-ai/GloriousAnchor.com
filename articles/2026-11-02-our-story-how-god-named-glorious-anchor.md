> *Write the vision; make it plain on tablets, so he may run who reads it. For still the vision awaits its appointed time.* (Habakkuk 2:2-3)

I am Michael Ede, convener of Prevailing Prayer Mountain (PPM) and visioner of Glorious Anchor International Network (GAIN). What you are reading on this site did not begin as a media idea. It began as a word from God, spoken more than once, and I have kept the dates. I set them down here for our record, because we believe the set time for the manifestation of what the Lord promised is now imminent, and because "he may run who reads it".

## "Write": the night the name came

The encounters that birthed Glorious Anchor started on [day] August [year], at about [time] in the morning. The Lord spoke to me clearly three times. At the fourth instance He said, "WRITE", and I scrambled for something to write with and set down exactly what I heard: GLORIOUS ANCHOR.

Then He declared: **"There shall be manifestation of tornado of His power, riding in the wings of His glory."**

I had been an English teacher for some years before that night, and up to that date I had never used or, as far as I can remember, seen the word *tornado*. It was not my vocabulary. It was His.

## What "riding in the wings of His glory" means

On [day] August [year], at about [time] in the morning, while I was studying my Bible, the Spirit of the Lord ministered to me the meaning of the phrase: **"Riding in the wings of His glory means that what men struggle to achieve shall be accomplished with ease; what took men years will take a twinkling of an eye in Glorious Anchor."**

That is the promise over everything that carries this name: not striving, but glory doing in a moment what effort could not do in years.

## The broom and the globe: Shiloh, Benin City

On [day] December [year], at Winners Chapel, Sapele Road, Benin City, during Shiloh, the yearly gathering of Winners worldwide, the Spirit of the Lord ministered to me to clean up the church premises at the break of dawn. Neglecting shame, I did it diligently and joyfully.

The next day, [day] December, still on the Shiloh ground, I had a strong and irresistible prompting to get a globe. So during the break I went with urgency to Ring Road, the city centre of Benin, to buy one, with the last cash I had on me on that mountain. As soon as I bought the globe I had my peace back, and He spoke to me clearly: **"Glorious Anchor is raised for world impact and will lend unto nations. I am raising her as a voice."**

The confirmation came the same day. From the Shiloh pulpit, Bishop David Oyedepo declared that God is raising people and ministers to affect the world with great impact and to fly higher than he has flown in his own lifetime.

## The light in the room

On Wednesday [day] January [year], from [time] in the morning and for several hours, God's presence was so strong in my room after study and prayer. I was on a [number]-day fast. I saw light all over the room, on the roof and on the walls, and then suddenly the light entered my side and I felt the vibration physically. I stood up praying in the Holy Ghost.

## Goshen, the budgets of nations, and an apostle of light

On [day] March [year], the Lord told me that Glorious Anchor is **Goshen City: a city of light, a revelation centre.** Goshen was the place in Egypt where there was light in the Israelites' houses while darkness lay over everything else (Exodus 10:23).

On [day] June [year], at about [time] in the morning, God told me that **the projects and bills of Glorious Anchor will appear in the budgets of governments, institutions and businesses, and in the free-will offerings and sacrifices of individuals.**

And on [day] April [year], while I was in service at Winners Chapel, Sapele Road, Benin City, I heard God say: **"I have ordained you an apostle of light."**

## Why I have written the dates

The reason for dating and listing these encounters was, and is, for our record. We believe the set time is now imminent for the manifestation of the things the Lord has promised, and a written record is how a people run with a vision (Habakkuk 2:2). When what He said comes to pass, it will be seen that He said it first.

I became the convener of Prevailing Breakthrough Prayer Mountain Ministry after pastoring at Living Faith Church, also known as Winners Chapel International. Glorious Anchor International Network, with its news service, GAIN TV and GAIN Radio, is one expression of the voice He said He was raising. If you have read this far, you are part of the record too. Pray with us, and watch.

## Questions this article answers

### Who is Michael Ede?

Michael Ede is the convener of Prevailing Prayer Mountain (PPM) and the visioner of Glorious Anchor International Network (GAIN). He pastored at Living Faith Church (Winners Chapel International) before founding the prayer mountain ministry, and was an English teacher before that.

### What is Glorious Anchor International Network?

GAIN is the network God named "Glorious Anchor" in a series of encounters recorded here, which He said He was "raising as a voice" for "world impact". Today it includes the Glorious Anchor news and teaching service, GAIN TV and GAIN Radio.

### What does "tornado of His power riding in the wings of His glory" mean?

It was the first word God spoke over Glorious Anchor. He later explained that "riding in the wings of His glory" means what men struggle to achieve will be accomplished with ease, and what took men years will take a twinkling of an eye.

### Why is Glorious Anchor called Goshen City?

Because the Lord said so: "Glorious Anchor is Goshen City, a city of light, a revelation centre." Goshen was the district of Egypt where the Israelites had light while the rest of the land was dark (Exodus 10:23).

### What is Shiloh at Winners Chapel?

Shiloh is the annual gathering of Living Faith Church (Winners Chapel) members worldwide, held each December. Two of the encounters recorded here happened on the Shiloh ground at Winners Chapel, Sapele Road, Benin City.
