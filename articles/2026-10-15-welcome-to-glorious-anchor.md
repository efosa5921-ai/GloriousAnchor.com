*We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure.* — Hebrews 6:19

There is more good Christian writing, reporting and teaching being published every day than any of us can keep up with. It is scattered across news sites, ministry blogs, Substacks, YouTube channels and social feeds, each with its own app, its own login, its own notifications.

Glorious Anchor gathers it into one calm page. Headlines from trusted outlets sit next to long-form essays, daily devotionals, new sermons and the conversations happening in Christian communities online. Nothing is written by an algorithm, and nothing is hidden behind one. Every card links straight to the original author.

## How to use it

- **Top Stories** is a balanced mix of everything, refreshed through the day.
- **My Feed** lets you follow only the publishers, writers and channels you want. Your choices stay on your device.
- **Picks** are links we chose by hand, with a note on why.
- **Our Articles**, this section, is where we publish our own writing.

On a phone, use your browser's *Add to Home Screen* to install Glorious Anchor as an app. It keeps the latest headlines for reading offline.

## A note on sources

We link, we do not copy. Headlines, summaries and images belong to the people who made them, and we show only what they publish in their own public feeds. If you publish something you think belongs here, or would rather not be included, we would love to hear from you.

Thank you for reading. Stay anchored.
