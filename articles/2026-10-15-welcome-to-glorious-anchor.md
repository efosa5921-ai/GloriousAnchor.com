*We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure.* — Hebrews 6:19

There is more good Christian reporting, writing and teaching published every day than any of us can keep up with. It is scattered across news sites, ministry blogs, Substacks, YouTube channels and social feeds, each with its own app, its own login and its own notifications.

Glorious Anchor gathers it into one calm page. Nothing here is written by an algorithm and nothing is hidden behind one. Every card links straight to the person who made it.

## What you will find

- **Top Stories** is a balanced mix of everything below, refreshed through the day, with no single publisher allowed to crowd out the rest.
- **News** carries Christian news outlets from around the world.
- **Faith & Culture** holds essays and commentary from magazines and journals.
- **Teaching & Devotional** brings daily teaching and devotionals from trusted ministries.
- **Apologetics** gathers writers and channels that explain and defend the faith.
- **Eschatology** follows teaching on the end times and biblical prophecy.
- **Writers & Blogs** collects individual authors, including their newsletters.
- **YouTube** shows new videos from teaching, apologetics and worship channels.
- **Social** brings in conversations from Christian communities online.
- **Picks** are links we chose by hand, with a note on why they are worth your time.
- **Our Articles**, this section, is where we publish our own writing.

Use the search box to look across every headline, video and post at once. Type `/` on a keyboard to jump straight to it.

## Make it yours

**My Feed** lets you follow only the publishers, writers, channels and communities you want. Choose them once with the source picker and the tab shows just those. Your choices stay on your device; there is no account and no login.

The **bookmark** on any card saves it for later under *Saved*, again only on your device.

## Find a church near you

The **Find a church** button opens a finder that locates Christian churches around you, or around any town you type, within a radius you choose. Each result shows the distance, denomination, address and, where known, service times and a website, with a one-tap link for directions. Churches listed directly with Glorious Anchor appear first with a gold *Listed* badge. The rest come from OpenStreetMap, the world's open map, so it works anywhere.

If your church is missing, you can add it to OpenStreetMap, which helps everyone, or ask us to list it.

## On your phone

Open the site in your phone's browser and choose *Add to Home Screen* (Safari) or *Install* (Chrome). Glorious Anchor then opens like an app, keeps the latest headlines for reading offline, and tells you when a new version is ready. Home-screen shortcuts jump straight to My Feed, the church finder and your saved items.

## A note on sources

We link, we do not copy. Headlines, summaries and images belong to the people who made them, and we show only what they publish in their own public feeds, with a link back to the source. The site sets no tracking cookies and shows no advertising. If you publish something you think belongs here, or would rather not be included, we would love to hear from you.

Thank you for reading. Stay anchored.
