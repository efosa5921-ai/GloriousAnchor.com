*We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure.* — Hebrews 6:19

There is more good Christian reporting, writing and teaching published every day than any of us can keep up with. It is scattered across news sites, ministry blogs, Substacks, YouTube channels and social feeds, each with its own app, its own login and its own notifications.

Glorious Anchor gathers it into one calm page, with a heart for the Spirit-filled, Pentecostal and evangelistic church: ministries that preach the gospel, pray for the sick and expect the Holy Spirit to move today. Nothing here is written by an algorithm and nothing is hidden behind one. Every card links straight to the person who made it.

## What you will find

Top Stories opens with **From our writers**, a slider of our own articles that turns to a different one every two hours; swipe or use the arrows to read the others.

- **Top Stories** is a balanced mix of everything below, refreshed through the day, with no single publisher allowed to crowd out the rest.
- **News** carries Christian news outlets from around the world.
- **Faith & Culture** holds essays and commentary from magazines and journals.
- **Teaching & Devotional** brings daily teaching and devotionals from trusted ministries.
- **Apologetics** gathers writers and channels that explain and defend the faith.
- **Eschatology** follows teaching on the end times and biblical prophecy.

Every section also has its own address, such as gloriousanchor.com/eschatology/ or gloriousanchor.com/news/, listed in the footer of every page, so you can bookmark or share one section on its own.
- **Prophecy in the News** reads current events in the light of Scripture: our own articles on what is happening in the world and what the Bible says about it, alongside prophecy ministries.
- **The Holy Spirit** brings news and teaching on the Spirit-filled life, from Pentecostal and charismatic publishers.
- **Writers & Blogs** collects individual authors, including their newsletters.
- **YouTube** shows new videos from teaching, apologetics and worship channels.
- **Radio** gathers Christian podcasts and audio teaching. Press Listen and the episode plays right there on the card.
- **Social** brings in conversations from Christian communities online.
- **Picks** are links we chose by hand, with a note on why they are worth your time.
- **Our Articles**, this section, is where we publish our own writing.

Use the search box to look across every headline, video and post at once. Type `/` on a keyboard to jump straight to it.

## GAIN TV and GAIN Radio

Glorious Anchor is part of the Glorious Anchor International Network, GAIN for short. Two of its channels live here as their own front doors: [GAIN TV](/tv/) opens straight onto the video section, and [GAIN Radio](/radio/) opens onto the podcasts. They have their own addresses too: gaintv.app and gainradio.app bring you straight here. Everything else on the site is one tap away from either.

## Make it yours

**My Feed** lets you follow only the publishers, writers, channels and communities you want. Choose them once with the source picker and the tab shows just those. Your choices stay on your device; there is no account and no login.

The **bookmark** on any card saves it for later under *Saved*, again only on your device.

## Find a church near you

The **Find a church** button opens a finder that locates Christian churches around you, or around any town you type, within a radius you choose. Each result shows the distance, denomination, address and, where known, service times and a website, with a one-tap link for directions. Churches listed directly with Glorious Anchor appear first with a gold *Listed* badge. The rest come from OpenStreetMap, the world's open map, so it works anywhere.

If your church is missing, you can add it to OpenStreetMap, which helps everyone, or ask us to list it.

## Test yourself

The [Bible Quiz](/quiz/) sets a new quiz of the day, ten questions with four answers each. Every answer shows the reference so you can read the passage yourself, and at the end you get a score to share with your group and dare them to beat it.

## Pass it on

Every card has a share button: send a story to a WhatsApp group, post it to X, Facebook or Telegram, email it, or copy the link. Whoever opens it lands on the story here, one tap from the full article at its source, with more from the same section alongside. On a phone it opens your own share sheet. The [daily digest](/digest/), in the tab bar beside Find a church, gathers the day's best stories in one page you can copy straight into a group. Put your email address in the subscribe box and the digest comes to your inbox every morning, or follow the [WhatsApp channel](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbDsSvP2Jl86RiDqNe3d) and the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/gloriousanchor), where new stories arrive through the day. If you prefer a feed reader, [/feed.xml](/feed.xml) carries the latest items.

## On your phone

Open the site in your phone's browser and choose *Add to Home Screen* (Safari) or *Install* (Chrome). Glorious Anchor then opens like an app, keeps the latest headlines for reading offline, and tells you when a new version is ready. Home-screen shortcuts jump straight to My Feed, the church finder and your saved items.

## A note on sources

The publishers, ministries, channels and podcasts we draw from lean charismatic, Pentecostal and evangelistic, alongside the main Christian news outlets. We link, we do not copy. Headlines, summaries and images belong to the people who made them, and we show only what they publish in their own public feeds, with a link back to the source. The site sets no tracking cookies and shows no advertising. If you publish something you think belongs here, or would rather not be included, write to [info@gloriousanchor.com](mailto:info@gloriousanchor.com). We would love to hear from you.

## Questions this article answers

### What is Glorious Anchor?

Glorious Anchor is a free Christian news and media site that gathers news, teaching, articles, sermons, worship, podcasts and social posts from charismatic, Pentecostal and evangelistic ministries and the main Christian news outlets into one fast page, with a church finder, a daily digest and a Bible quiz.

### What are GAIN TV and GAIN Radio?

GAIN TV ([gaintv.app](https://gaintv.app)) is the video side of the network, with new Christian videos every day, and GAIN Radio ([gainradio.app](https://gainradio.app)) is the audio side, with podcasts and broadcasts that play on the page. Both are part of the Glorious Anchor International Network.

### Is Glorious Anchor free, and does it track me?

Yes, it is free, and no, it sets no tracking cookies and shows no advertising. You can follow sources, save stories and use My Feed without an account.

### How do I get Glorious Anchor on my phone?

Open gloriousanchor.com in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It then opens like an app. You can also follow the WhatsApp channel, Telegram channel or the daily email digest.
