# FESTAC 77: The Lease — shot list for AI-generated clips

Each `###` block is one clip for Google Veo (8 seconds, 16:9, native sound). `chars:` names the character
portraits passed as reference images so faces stay the same from clip to clip. The prompt body is sent as written,
after the house style line. Spoken lines are in double quotes; Veo voices them. The narrator is NOT generated here:
`scripts/article-video.js` lays the narration (`narration:` lines) over the finished clips.

style: Cinematic live-action drama, shot on 35mm film, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, realistic Nigerian people and places, period-accurate detail. Natural ambient sound. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermark.

negative: subtitles, captions, text overlay, watermark, cartoon, animation, CGI look, distorted faces, extra fingers, modern smartphones in 1970s scenes
